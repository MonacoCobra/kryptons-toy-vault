# Figure backlog rules (Shelby 2026-09-05)

- **Release floor:** 1980-01-01. Catalog modern → 1980; nothing older than 1980.
- **Brand universe:** `company-universe.txt` — full BigBadToyStore A–Z brand list (source of truth for which makers exist in the hobby).
- **Segment filter:** articulated **action figures** only. From the BBTS universe, exclude board games, RPG pubs, comics publishers, music labels, apparel, lamps, cards-only, etc. Prefer figure/toy makers (Hasbro, Mattel, NECA, Mezco, Bandai/Tamashii, Kaiyodo, Super7, Boss Fight, Hiya, Mondo, Hot Toys, McFarlane, Diamond Select, Jakks, threezero, Storm Collectibles, Takara Tomy, …).
- **Images:** no generative AI art; CSS/palette placeholders OK.
- **Cadence:** inject batches like comics (~every 3 weeks with N&N promotion).
